# Business Model Documentation

## Executive Summary

The FinTech-AgTech Credit Scoring System operates as a **collective project financing platform** that enables farmer groups to access loans for shared agricultural ventures. The platform generates revenue through industry-standard fees while providing behavioral-based credit scoring to underbanked farmers.

## 🎯 Value Proposition

### **For Farmers**
- Access to credit without traditional collateral requirements
- Group-based risk sharing and social support
- Profit sharing from successful collective projects
- Skill development through courses and tasks
- Progressive advancement through badge system

### **For Lenders/Partners**
- Reduced risk through group-based lending model
- Behavioral data for enhanced risk assessment
- Specialized agricultural market expertise
- Technology-enabled monitoring and reporting
- Proven track record with performance analytics

### **For the Platform**
- Sustainable revenue through industry-standard fees
- Scalable technology infrastructure
- Deep agricultural market knowledge
- Strong farmer community engagement

## 💰 Revenue Model

### **Primary Revenue Streams**

#### **1. Loan Origination Fees**
- **Platform Share**: 60-70% of total origination fees
- **Partner Share**: 30-40% of total origination fees
- **Industry Standard**: 2-8% of loan amount
- **Example**: ₦100,000 loan × 5% fee = ₦5,000 total fee
  - Platform keeps: ₦3,000-3,500
  - Partner keeps: ₦1,500-2,000

#### **2. Grant Administration Fees**
- **Platform Share**: 80-90% of administration fees
- **Partner Share**: 10-20% of administration fees
- **Typical Range**: 5-15% of grant amount
- **Example**: $1,000 grant × 10% fee = $100 total fee
  - Platform keeps: $80-90
  - Partner keeps: $10-20

#### **3. One-Time Membership Fees**
- **Nigeria (Local)**: ₦5,000-15,000 (admin configurable)
- **International**: $10-30 (admin configurable)
- **Payment**: Single payment, no recurring subscriptions
- **Access**: Required for financial services

#### **4. Group Entry Fees**
- **Nigeria**: ₦10,000-25,000 (tiered based on group benefits)
- **International**: $20-50 (tiered based on group benefits)
- **Payment**: One-time when joining a group
- **Constraint**: Users can only be in one group at a time

#### **5. Course Commission Fees**
- **Platform Share**: 20-25% of course fees
- **Provider Share**: 75-80% of course fees
- **Source**: External course platform partnerships
- **Trigger**: Successful course completion with certificate verification
